# Gemini prompt — Food Run fish skin art

Paste everything below the line into Gemini (image generation). Generate one skin
per request for the most consistent results, swapping the skin description each time.

---

You are creating game-ready 2D fish sprites for "Food Run: Beneath the Current,"
a polished underwater stealth game with a colorful, soft-gradient, flat-shaded
art style — think modern indie game, not realistic and not pixel art.

**Hard requirements (identical for every image):**
- A single small reef fish in strict side view, facing LEFT, centered.
- Full body visible: oval body, one large side fin, forked or fan tail, one big
  readable eye with a pale ring around a dark pupil.
- Same pose, proportions, and framing every time: the fish fills about 70% of a
  square canvas.
- Transparent background (PNG), 1024×1024. No water, no bubbles, no scenery,
  no text, no watermark.
- Smooth vector-like shapes with soft two-tone gradient shading and a single
  light source from the upper left. A subtle glossy highlight along the back.
- Clean silhouette — no fussy detail that would vanish at 48 pixels tall, since
  the sprite will be displayed small in-game.

**Skin to generate (swap this block per request):**

1. GOLDEN FISH (rare) — molten gold body with amber gradient, koi-like white
   patch on the belly, tail like beaten metal catching light.
2. NEON FISH (epic) — electric cyan-to-violet gradient body, thin glowing
   magenta pinstripes along the flank, fins like translucent neon glass.
3. BIOLUMINESCENT FISH (epic) — deep teal body scattered with tiny glowing
   spring-green photophore dots, fin edges emitting soft light.
4. GHOST FISH (legendary) — pale translucent white-blue body like frosted
   glass, faint inner glow where the spine would be, wispy trailing fin edges.
5. MIDNIGHT FISH (uncommon) — deep navy body with indigo gradient, small
   silver star-fleck scales, moon-pale belly.
6. TROPICAL FISH (uncommon) — bold vertical bands of mango yellow, seafoam
   green, and lagoon blue with an orange tail.
7. CORAL FISH (common) — warm coral-pink body fading to rose, cream belly,
   slightly ruffled fins like soft coral.

After generating, also produce a matching 5-color palette listing (hex codes)
for: body-light, body-mid, body-dark, tail/fin, belly — sampled from the image.
